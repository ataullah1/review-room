# Olive Café demonstration artwork

The four Olive Café WebP images were drawn for Review Room using original SVG geometry and fictional copy, then rasterized by `scripts/reviewer-demo/assets.ts`. No client material, downloaded photographs, third-party logo or external stock asset is used. Olive Café is a fictional sample concept, not a claimed customer.
